# My First Magic Website

Hello! I am 7 years old and this is my very first web design project.

## What this does:
1. It shows a welcome message.
2. It has an orange magic button.
3. When you click the button, the words turn red and change!

## Files in this project:
* **index.html** - The skeleton of the page.
* **styles.css** - The paint and colors.
* **scripts.js** - The magic that makes the button work.
